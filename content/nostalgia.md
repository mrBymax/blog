I don't know why I feel nostalgia for a period I did not even live.
I miss old Disney movies from the 50s, old jazz music from the 60s, old fashion, elegant style from the early 1900s.

And yet, I will never have the opportunity to feel and live something as beautiful as those things;
I've nostalgia for the movies I watched as a kid, from the memories I wish I had created growing up. I remember songs and sounds from cartoons that are older than my grandparents. 

Is the astonishing amount of mediocrity and boredom of the present times that pushes me back in time?
